## Design Context

### Users
Recruiters, hiring managers, and potential clients in Egypt's tech/marketing industry. Viewing on desktop during work hours, evaluating Youssef's skills and experience for data analyst roles.

### Brand Personality
**Warm, grounded, professional** — not flashy tech-bro, not corporate sterile. Think: a well-prepared candidate who lets their work speak. Copper accent conveys warmth and craftsmanship over cold tech.

### Aesthetic Direction
**Editorial/portfolio** — dark background, warm copper accent, asymmetric layouts, strong typography hierarchy. Anti-references: AI startup landing pages, SaaS dashboards, crypto portfolios, anything with purple-cyan gradients.

### Design Principles
1. **Warmth over coldness** — copper tones, warm dark backgrounds, never sterile
2. **Content-first** — sections designed around what they contain, not template patterns
3. **Restraint** — one accent color used sparingly, no gradient text, no glassmorphism everywhere
4. **Distinctive typography** — clear hierarchy with varied weights, not flat sizing
5. **Intentional asymmetry** — left-aligned content, varied spacing, not everything centered
